Read text description: MapleWorks investment slide

Choose one improvement. Option A upgrades Cutting from 8 to 10 units per round for $40 per round. Option B adds Assembly support, increasing capacity from 5 to 7 for $60 per round. Option C upgrades Finishing from 7 to 9 for $40 per round.

The team must justify the additional finished units, additional contribution, net effect after cost and why it chose that option. Phase 3 allows 6 minutes to make the investment decision, then commit.
