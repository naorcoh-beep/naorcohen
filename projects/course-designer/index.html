Course Designer

An AI-powered teaching skill that reviews a course syllabus, identifies pedagogical strengths and areas for improvement, and helps instructors turn selected recommendations into ready-to-use teaching materials.

A syllabus review, followed through

An excerpt from the supplied illustrative review of an undergraduate Organizational Behaviour course.

The syllabus
Two exams and a staged group research project. The objectives mainly emphasise knowing and explaining concepts.
The review
The project asks students to evaluate research evidence, but that capability is not explicit in the objectives.
The instructor’s choice
Keep the assessments and weights. Develop a project-level learning objective.

Evaluate organizational practices using relevant organizational behaviour theories and credible research evidence, considering contextual factors, competing explanations, and potential unintended consequences.

Select one or more recommendations. The sample follows an instructor who keeps the assessments and weights unchanged, then develops a proposed objective and assessment map.

What to bring and what to expect

What to bring

A syllabus is enough to begin. Add an assignment brief, rubric or sample questions if you want a closer review of a particular issue.

The first review identifies strengths, up to three priorities and questions that need verification. Choose a priority to develop into materials such as revised objectives, an assessment map or assignment instructions. Keep the course constraints you need to preserve.

Suggestions remain drafts for your judgement and local approval. The skill does not change official course files.

Course Designer is a prototype, tried against course outlines. This does not establish classroom effectiveness.
