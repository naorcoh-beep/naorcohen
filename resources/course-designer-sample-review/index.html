Course Designer: sample review

An illustrative review and follow-up, showing how a course outline can lead to a focused improvement.

Illustrative review drawn from an undergraduate introductory Organizational Behaviour course outline. Not evidence of observed classroom effectiveness.

Input

A syllabus with two individual exams (30% each) and a team-based evidence-informed research project (proposal 5%, video 10%, report 25%). Teams formulate a PICOC question, receive feedback on the proposal, and assess organizational practices using research evidence. The official objectives mainly emphasize knowing and explaining OB concepts.

Short diagnostic review

Overall assessment: The course has a coherent foundation and a strong applied project. A full redesign is not warranted. The most valuable change is to make evidence-based judgement an explicit learning target.

Keep

  • Early introduction to evidence-based management, a library workshop, and a PICOC proposal.
  • A staged research project that requires analysis of research, practical applicability, and unintended consequences.
  • Individual exams (60%) complemented by collaborative work (40%).

Improve / verify

  1. Align objectives and assessments (change): The research project demands more than the stated knowledge-based objectives. Add an evidence-evaluation objective or revise existing objectives, subject to approval.
  2. Individual evidence of learning (possible improvement): With 40% group work, consider a short individual evidence explanation, but first determine whether exams or other activities already assess this capability.
  3. Feedback between proposal and final products (verify): The outline documents proposal feedback but does not establish whether additional feedback occurs. Add a midpoint checkpoint only if existing support is insufficient.

Assessment snapshot

Evidence Weight Primary contribution
Midterm 30% Individual understanding of foundational OB
Final exam 30% Individual understanding of later OB topics
Group proposal 5% Research question and search plan
Group video 10% Communicating findings
Group report 25% Critical evidence-based analysis

Explore improvements (choose any)

  1. Align objectives and assessments: Two routes, with a revised objective and assessment map.
  2. Make individual learning visible: An individual research explanation and a low-marking alternative.
  3. Clarify project feedback: A 10-minute evidence checkpoint and an option to verify current practice.
  4. Optional deeper review: Add the project rubric or sample exams.

Example instructor choice

Expand option 1. Keep the assessments and weights unchanged; prepare ready-to-paste English wording.

Example ready-to-use deliverable

Proposed project-level learning objective:

Evaluate organizational practices using relevant organizational behaviour theories and credible research evidence, considering contextual factors, competing explanations, and potential unintended consequences.

Proposed alignment

Assessment Weight Main learning evidence
Midterm examination 30% Core individual-level OB concepts
Final examination 30% Power, influence, and leadership concepts
Group proposal 5% Researchable question and evidence search plan
Group video 10% Communication of evidence-based findings
Group report 25% Critical evaluation and contextualized recommendations

Implementation note: Add this as a project-level outcome if official course outcomes cannot be changed without approval. The table is a proposed mapping, not proof of exam item-level alignment. Review actual questions and rubric before claiming full alignment.
