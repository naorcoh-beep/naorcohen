From Learning Goals to Evidence of Learning

Three connected AI-powered skills for thoughtful course design.

Good teaching begins with three questions: What should students learn? How can we help them learn? And how will we know they have learned?

  1. 1. Course Designer: What do we want students to learn?

    Clarify learning outcomes, organize course content, and align teaching with intended learning goals.

  2. 2. Active Learning Designer: How will we help students learn?

    Transform learning goals into engaging, ready-to-run activities, from simulations and role-plays to cases and collaborative exercises.

  3. 3. Assessment Architect: How will we know they have learned?

    Design meaningful assessments that reveal what students know, understand, and can do.

Better Together, Useful on Their Own

Together, the three skills connect learning goals, learning experiences, and evidence of learning, creating a coherent approach to course design.

Each skill also works independently. Use one to address a specific teaching challenge, or combine all three to design a complete learning experience.
