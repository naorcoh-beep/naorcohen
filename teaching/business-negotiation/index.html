Business Negotiation

OBHR 493 · OBHR 647 · Undergraduate and graduate

Students prepare and practise negotiations, then examine the decisions they made. They identify interests, priorities and reservation points, consider the other party’s perspective and choose strategies for reaching agreements and handling conflict.

I teach undergraduate OBHR 493 and graduate OBHR 647. The Summer 2023 graduate block-week offering combined participation, readings and quizzes with a self-appraisal paper about students’ strengths and weaknesses as negotiators. Its overview documents that offering, rather than the undergraduate course.
